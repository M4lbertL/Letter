I’ve waited long enough for my turn, and I’m not about to let this chance go. Like I’ve said before, my feelings only grow deeper the more I get to know you, and the more I do, the more I find myself wanting more.

More of us laughing our asses off at the game, more of the stories you tell me about your life, more of hearing your voice and how soft you can be with me, more of those days when it’s just us—more of you.
